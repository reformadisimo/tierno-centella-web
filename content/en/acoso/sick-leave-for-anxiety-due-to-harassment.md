If harassment causes you anxiety or depression, you are entitled to medical sick leave (baja médica). What matters is that it is recognised as a work accident (accidente de trabajo) and not as a common illness (enfermedad común), because you are paid more and sooner: 75 % of your benefit base (base reguladora) from the day after your leave begins. To achieve this, tell your doctor about the work-related cause from your very first appointment and, if necessary, ask the National Social Security Institute (INSS) to determine the type of contingency.

## Sick leave for anxiety is sick leave like any other

Anxiety, depression or stress that prevent you from working are grounds for sick leave just like a physical injury. You do not have to put up with it or justify yourself. A doctor decides, not your company.

Sick leave, which is technically called temporary incapacity (incapacidad temporal), can be of two types depending on its cause:

- **Common contingency (contingencia común):** common illness or a non-work accident.
- **Occupational contingency (contingencia profesional):** a work accident or an occupational disease (enfermedad profesional).

By default, if you go to your GP (médico de cabecera), the sick leave is usually issued as a common illness. If the cause is harassment, that is not correct, and it works against you.

## Why it matters that it is a work accident

Anxiety or depression caused by harassment are not on the official list of occupational diseases. That is why they are channelled as a work accident: the law treats as such the illnesses you contract exclusively because of your work.

The difference in what you are paid is real:

| | Work accident | Common illness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day the leave begins | Salary paid by the company | Salary paid by the company |
| Days 2 and 3 | 75 % | Nothing by law (unless improved by the collective agreement) |
| Days 4 to 20 | 75 % | 60 % |
| From day 21 | 75 % | 75 % |
| Prior contributions required | None | 180 days in the last 5 years |

The percentages are applied to your benefit base, which is similar, though not identical, to your gross salary. Many collective agreements (convenios) improve these figures, especially for work accidents.

But it is not only about money. Having it treated as a work accident also:

- Strengthens your harassment claim: an official body recognises that your illness comes from work.
- If the company did not prevent psychosocial risks, it opens the door to asking for a benefits surcharge (recargo de prestaciones).
- If you end up with a permanent disability, the calculation is more favourable and no prior contributions are required. More information in [permanent disability](/en/permanent-disability).

> Whether your sick leave counts as a work accident does not depend on what the company says. If it refuses to recognise it, you can request it yourself.

## What to tell your doctor

The first report is key. Anything not recorded there will be harder to prove later.

- **Explain the cause.** Say clearly that your distress comes from what is happening to you at work. Briefly describe the facts: humiliation, isolation, shouting, since when.
- **Describe your symptoms.** Insomnia, anxiety about going to work, crying, difficulty concentrating, physical symptoms.
- **Ask for the link to your work to be recorded** in your medical history.
- **Be honest.** Do not exaggerate or hide other sources of stress. Credibility is your strongest card.

If you are referred to mental health services, keep every report. They are important evidence. We explain the rest in [how to prove harassment](/en/workplace-harassment/how-to-prove-workplace-harassment).

## How to ask for it to be recognised as a work accident

### First, the mutual insurer

If your company covers occupational contingencies with a mutual insurer (mutua), you can go to it and explain that your condition comes from work. Very often the mutual insurer rejects it and sends you back to your GP. Always ask for that rejection in writing.

### Then, the procedure to determine the contingency

You can ask the National Social Security Institute (Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad Social, INSS) to decide whether your sick leave is common or occupational. You can start this yourself. Provide the medical reports and any evidence of the harassment you have.

If the INSS refuses, you can file a prior administrative claim (reclamación previa) and then a claim in the Social Court (Juzgado de lo Social).

> The prior administrative claim against the INSS decision must be filed within 30 working days (días hábiles) of being notified. Check your dates in [deadlines](/herramientas/plazos) (in Spanish).

This procedure does not stop your sick leave: while it is being decided, you remain on leave and are paid as for a common illness. If the decision goes in your favour, the differences are paid to you.

## While you are on sick leave

- **You are not obliged to talk to the company** about your health or to answer calls from the harasser.
- **Do what your doctor recommends.** Going for walks, going out or seeing your family is not incompatible with sick leave for anxiety, as long as it does not harm your recovery. Avoid activities that contradict your diagnosis.
- **Attend appointments** with the mutual insurer or the INSS. Not going may lead to your benefit being suspended.
- **Keep gathering evidence** of what happened while it is fresh in your mind.

## If you are dismissed while on sick leave

It happens more than you might think. Dismissing someone on sick leave is not automatically void (nulo), but there are strong protections:

- Since Law 15/2022 on equal treatment (Ley 15/2022, de igualdad de trato), illness or health condition is a ground of discrimination. A dismissal motivated by your sick leave can be declared void.
- If you are dismissed after complaining about the harassment or after asking for the contingency to be determined, it may be retaliation. The law also protects you against that (protection against retaliation, garantía de indemnidad).
- If the dismissal is void, the company must reinstate you and pay you the wages you did not receive. You can also claim damages.

You have 20 working days to challenge the dismissal, and August counts. Do not wait until you are signed back to work (alta) to take action. More information in [dismissal](/en/dismissal).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can my company find out why I am on sick leave?

No. The sick note (parte) the company receives does not include the diagnosis. Only you decide what to tell them.

### Can the mutual insurer put me on sick leave directly?

If the mutual insurer recognises the work-related cause, yes, it manages the leave. If it rejects it, your GP puts you on leave as a common illness and you can ask for the contingency to be determined.

### Can I ask for it to be changed to a work accident if the leave started as a common illness?

Yes. The procedure exists precisely for that, even if the leave is already under way.

### What happens when I am signed back to work?

You will have to return to work. If the harassment continues, there are ways out without losing your rights; we explain them in [quitting your job because of harassment](/en/workplace-harassment/quitting-your-job-because-of-harassment).
