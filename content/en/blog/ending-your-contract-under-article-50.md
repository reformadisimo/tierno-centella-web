Article 50 of the Workers' Statute (Estatuto de los Trabajadores) allows you to ask the judge to end your contract because of a serious breach by the company and to award you compensation as if you had been unfairly dismissed (despido improcedente): 33 days' salary per year worked, up to a maximum of 24 months' pay. It is the way out when you cannot go on any longer, but leaving on your own would make you lose your compensation and your unemployment benefit (paro).

> Check it with [the "Can I leave with compensation?" test](/en/tools/leave-with-compensation-test).

## When you can ask for it

- **They pay you late or do not pay you**, on an ongoing basis.
- **They change your conditions** (duties, working hours, salary) in a way that harms your professional development or your dignity.
- **Any other serious breach** by the company, such as **workplace harassment** or failing to protect you from it, or refusing to reinstate you after a court ruling.

A case from our own firm in 2025: a worker who was demoted after asking for leave to care for her sick partner obtained the termination of her contract with compensation. We tell the story in [rulings](/en/rulings).

## How it is done

1. A conciliation claim (papeleta de conciliación) is filed, followed by the claim at the Social Court (Juzgado de lo Social).
2. **In the meantime, you keep working.** The employment relationship ends when the judgment says so.
3. If staying in the job puts your dignity, your health or your integrity at risk, as in harassment, you can ask the judge to allow you to stop going in while the proceedings last, while keeping your salary.

This point is delicate: stopping going in on your own without authorisation can end in a dismissal for absences. Always do it with a lawyer.

## What you get

- The unfair dismissal compensation.
- If your fundamental rights have also been breached, as in harassment or discrimination, additional compensation for damages.
- Unemployment benefit, because ending the contract this way is a legal situation of unemployment (situación legal de desempleo).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many late payslips does it take?

There is no exact number. Judges assess whether the delays are ongoing and significant, not a one-off delay.

### Can I ask for it if I am on sick leave?

Yes. Being on sick leave does not prevent you from asking for the contract to be ended because of a serious breach.

### What if the company dismisses me in the meantime?

The two sets of proceedings can be joined. If the dismissal is a reprisal for having asked for the termination, it may be void (nulo).

### How long does it take?

It depends on the court: from several months to more than a year. That is why it is important to weigh up carefully whether it is better to keep working or to ask for the interim measure (medida cautelar).
