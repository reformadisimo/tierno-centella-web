Yes, you can start without "perfect" evidence: hardly anyone has it at the beginning. In a harassment trial you do not have to prove everything yourself. It is enough to provide serious **indications** (indicios) that your rights are being breached, and then it is the company that has to explain, with objective reasons, that this is not the case. What you do need is to start gathering those indications from today.

> Not sure whether what is happening to you is harassment? Take [the workplace harassment test](/en/tools/harassment-test) and see what evidence you already have with the [evidence map](/en/tools/harassment-evidence-map).

## Why you do not need to have everything proven

In trials for breach of fundamental rights, such as harassment, the law shares the burden of proof (carga de la prueba) differently from usual. Articles 96.1 and 181.2 of the Social Jurisdiction Act (Ley Reguladora de la Jurisdicción Social) say so: if the worker provides well-founded indications of the breach, it is for the company to provide an objective and reasonable justification for its decisions.

Put simply: you have to show that there is smoke. The company has to prove that there is no fire.

An indication is not a suspicion. It is a fact that can be checked and that points in one direction: an unexplained change of duties straight after a complaint, a series of emails in a humiliating tone, a period of sick leave that the doctor links to work.

## What counts as an indication

- **Your diary of events**: date, time, place, what happened, who was present. Written when it happens, not months later. On its own it is not enough, but it puts everything else in order and gives credibility to your account.
- **Messages and emails**: keep them with their dates. A screenshot with no context is worth less than the full conversation.
- **Changes in your conditions**: duties taken away, changes of shift or location, exclusion from meetings or training, appraisals that suddenly get worse. These usually leave a paper trail.
- **Your written communication to the company**: if you report it in writing and the company does nothing, that is also an indication, because it was obliged to activate its protocol.
- **Medical reports**: what you tell your GP (médico de cabecera) or your psychologist is recorded with a date.
- **Witnesses**: coworkers who have seen something, even if today they do not dare to testify. Write down who they are.
- **Recordings of conversations you take part in**: in general they are valid. We explain it in [recording conversations to prove harassment](/en/blog/recording-conversations-to-prove-harassment).

None of these things is usually enough on its own. Together they draw a pattern, and harassment is precisely that: a pattern.

## What you can do from today

1. **Start the diary today**, even if the harassment goes back a long way. Reconstruct what happened before as precisely as you can and keep writing things down day by day.
2. **Make a copy of what you already have** outside the company's equipment: emails, messages, appraisals. Only your own material and what has been sent to you; do not take other people's documents or confidential documents.
3. **Tell your doctor** if it is affecting your health. It is not only about looking after yourself: it is recorded.
4. **Report it in writing to the company** or through its whistleblowing channel (canal de denuncias), calmly and specifically. If you are unsure how to do it, it is better with a lawyer: the first written document shapes everything else.
5. **Consider going to the Labour Inspectorate** (Inspección de Trabajo): it can investigate and its report carries a lot of weight.

## What you should not do

- Wait to "have more evidence" while the deadlines run.
- Record conversations you are not part of, or go into other people's email.
- Talk about it on social media or in coworkers' groups: it can turn against you.
- Sign anything they give you at that moment without reading it calmly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I file a complaint with only my word?

You can file a complaint or a conciliation claim (papeleta), but at trial your word alone is rarely enough. That is why the diary, medical reports and any written trace are so important: they turn your account into indications.

### What if my coworkers do not want to testify?

It is very common, out of fear of reprisals. Write down who saw it anyway. A lawyer will assess whether and how to call them, and sometimes there are other ways to prove the same thing.

### How much time do I have?

It depends on what you are claiming. For compensation for damages, generally one year, which in harassment cases usually counts from when it stops. If you are dismissed, only 20 working days (días hábiles). Check your dates with the [deadlines](/en/tools/deadlines) tool.

### Can they dismiss me for reporting?

A dismissal in retaliation for reporting harassment can be declared void (nulo), which forces the company to take you back. We explain it in [when a dismissal is void](/en/dismissal/when-is-a-dismissal-void).
