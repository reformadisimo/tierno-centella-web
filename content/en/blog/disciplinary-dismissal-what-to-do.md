A disciplinary dismissal (despido disciplinario) is one that the company justifies on the basis of serious misconduct on your part: absences, disobedience, poor performance, offensive behaviour or breach of good faith (transgresión de la buena fe). If the company cannot prove that misconduct, or the letter does not meet the requirements, the dismissal is unfair (improcedente) and you are entitled to compensation. And if it is in fact retaliation or discrimination, it is void (nulo).

> You have 20 working days (días hábiles) to claim, from the day after the dismissal. Work out your last day with [the deadlines calculator](/en/tools/deadlines).

## First things first: what to sign

Sign the letter only as received and write next to it **"received, not in agreement" (recibí, no conforme)** with the date. Signing for receipt does not mean you agree, but it is best to make that clear. Do not sign any voluntary resignation (baja voluntaria) or agreement document until a lawyer has seen it. The same goes for the final settlement (finiquito): [we explain why "not in agreement"](/en/blog/signing-the-final-settlement-not-in-agreement).

## What the letter must contain

- The **specific facts** you are accused of and the **date** on which they happened. A generic letter ("continued poor performance") leaves you without a defence and usually ends in the dismissal being found unfair.
- The **effective date** of the dismissal.
- That those facts are not **time-barred** (prescritos): minor offences are time-barred after 10 days, serious ones after 20 and very serious ones after 60 from when the company becomes aware of them, and in any case 6 months after they were committed.
- If you are a workers' representative, or a union member and the company knows it, additional steps (a formal disciplinary file, expediente contradictorio, or a hearing for the union delegates).

With [check your dismissal letter](/en/tools/check-your-dismissal-letter) you can look for the most common defects.

## Did they hear you before dismissing you?

Since the Supreme Court (Tribunal Supremo) ruling of 18 November 2024, before a disciplinary dismissal the company must give you the chance to defend yourself against the charges. If it did not, that is one more argument in your claim. We explain it in [the hearing before a disciplinary dismissal](/en/blog/hearing-before-a-disciplinary-dismissal).

## What you are paid

- **Final settlement**: your salary for the month, untaken holiday and the share of extra payments. Always, even if the dismissal is fair (procedente). Work it out with [the final settlement calculator](/en/tools/final-settlement-calculator).
- **Unemployment benefit** (paro): yes, disciplinary dismissal gives you the right to unemployment benefit. Apply within the following 15 working days. [Work out how much you are entitled to](/en/tools/unemployment-benefit-calculator).
- **Compensation**: in a disciplinary dismissal the company pays nothing. But if the judge declares it unfair, it is 33 days' salary per year worked, up to 24 months' pay. [Work out your compensation](/en/tools/compensation-calculator).

## When it is void

If you are dismissed for having made a claim, reported harassment, requested reduced working hours, being pregnant or being ill, the dismissal can be void even if the letter talks about misconduct. The consequence is reinstatement and the lost wages. See it in [when a dismissal is void](/en/dismissal/when-is-a-dismissal-void).

## The steps

1. Sign as "received, not in agreement".
2. Keep the letter, your payslips and the messages from the previous weeks.
3. Apply for unemployment benefit.
4. File the conciliation claim (papeleta de conciliación) before 20 working days have passed. We tell you how in [the conciliation claim](/en/blog/conciliation-claim-smac-after-dismissal).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I get unemployment benefit if I am dismissed on disciplinary grounds?

Yes. Disciplinary dismissal is a legal situation of unemployment (situación legal de desempleo), even if you do not agree and even if you challenge it.

### Can they dismiss me for being late?

Only if the lateness is repeated, unjustified and proven. A one-off delay does not justify a disciplinary dismissal.

### What happens if the letter does not give the date of the facts?

It is a significant defect. If you do not know what you are accused of or when, you cannot defend yourself, and judges usually declare the dismissal unfair.

### Can I refuse to sign the letter?

You can, but the company can prove delivery with witnesses or by burofax (a certified letter service). It is better to sign as "received, not in agreement".
