What you are paid on sick leave (baja) depends on its cause. If it is a **common illness** (enfermedad común), you are paid nothing for the first three days (unless your collective agreement improves on this), from day 4 to day 20 you receive 60% of your benefit base (base reguladora) and from day 21, 75%. If it is a **work accident or occupational disease** (accidente de trabajo o enfermedad profesional), you receive 75% from the day after your sick leave starts.

> That is why it matters so much that sick leave for anxiety caused by harassment is recognised as a work accident. Check it with [the work-related sick leave test](/en/tools/work-related-sick-leave-test).

## What you are paid, at a glance

| Days of sick leave | Common illness or non-work accident | Work accident or occupational disease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1 to 3 | Nothing, unless the collective agreement improves on this | 75% (from the day after) |
| Days 4 to 20 | 60% | 75% |
| From day 21 | 75% | 75% |

The **benefit base** is, in general, the contribution base (base de cotización) for the month before the sick leave divided by the number of days in that month. Many collective agreements (convenios colectivos) require the company to top up to 100% of salary: check yours.

## Who pays you

For a common illness, days 4 to 15 are paid by the company and, from day 16, by Social Security or the mutual insurance company (mutua), although the company normally keeps paying it to you in your payslip. For a work accident, the mutual insurance company pays from the start.

## How long it can last

- Up to **365 days**.
- It can be extended by **another 180 days** if you are expected to recover within that time.
- When you reach one year, control passes to the INSS (Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad Social), which can sign you off as fit (dar el alta), extend the sick leave or start a **permanent disability** (incapacidad permanente) file.

If you cannot go back to your job when your sick leave ends, see [the degrees of permanent disability](/en/permanent-disability/degrees-of-permanent-disability).

## Can they dismiss me while I am on sick leave?

They can dismiss you, but if the real reason is your illness, the dismissal can be void (nulo) since Law 15/2022 (Ley 15/2022). We explain it in [dismissal during sick leave](/en/blog/dismissal-during-sick-leave).

## Frequently asked questions

### Am I paid 100% while on sick leave for a common illness?

Social Security pays 60% from day 4 to day 20 and 75% from day 21. You only receive 100% if your collective agreement obliges the company to make up the difference. Many do, sometimes only from a certain day or with limits: check yours.

### Do I receive my extra payments while on sick leave?

The benefit already includes the proportional share of the extra payments (pagas extra), because the contribution base includes them spread over the year. If your collective agreement improves sick pay, there may be specific rules.

### Can I go on holiday or leave the house while on sick leave?

You can do whatever does not harm your recovery or contradict your diagnosis. What you cannot do is work or carry out activities incompatible with sick leave.

### What do I do if I am signed off as fit and I am not well?

You can challenge the discharge (alta) within a short deadline. If it comes from the INSS or the mutual insurance company, you have only a few days: get advice as soon as possible.

### Is sick leave for anxiety due to harassment a work accident?

It can be if it is shown that the cause is work. You have to request a determination of the cause (determinación de contingencia). Read [sick leave for anxiety due to harassment](/en/workplace-harassment/sick-leave-for-anxiety-due-to-harassment).
