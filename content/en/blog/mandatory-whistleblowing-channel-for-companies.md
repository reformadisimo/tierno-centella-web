Law 2/2023 on the protection of people who report breaches (Ley 2/2023, de protección de las personas que informan sobre infracciones) requires companies with **50 or more employees** and the entire public sector to have an **internal reporting channel** (canal interno de información): a secure and confidential way to report wrongdoing. Anyone who reports in good faith is protected from retaliation, including dismissal.

## What can be reported

- Criminal offences and serious or very serious administrative breaches.
- Breaches of European Union law.
- Breaches relating to employment and to health and safety at work, including harassment.

## How it protects you

- **Confidentiality** of your identity. Anonymous reports are also accepted.
- **Ban on retaliation**: dismissal, disciplinary sanctions, changes of duties, negative appraisals. If they happen, they are presumed to be retaliation and the company has to prove otherwise.
- In addition, in employment matters the **guarantee against retaliation** (garantía de indemnidad) still applies: if you are dismissed for reporting, the dismissal is void (nulo).

## Something we see at the firm

More and more often, the person carrying out the harassment uses the whistleblowing channel to report their victim first and present themselves as the injured party. If this happens to you, gather the evidence of what you have been suffering as soon as possible and do not respond without advice.

## If you are a company

As well as the channel, every company must have a **protocol against sexual and sex-based harassment** (protocolo frente al acoso sexual y por razón de sexo), and must assess psychosocial risks. We help design protocols and investigate complaints impartially: [services for companies](/empresas).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does my company with 30 employees have to have a whistleblowing channel?

It is not required to by Law 2/2023, unless it belongs to certain regulated sectors. But it must have a harassment protocol and assess psychosocial risks.

### Can I report harassment through the whistleblowing channel?

Yes, and also through the harassment protocol. What matters is that it is in writing and dated.

### What if I am sanctioned after using the channel?

The sanction can be overturned as retaliation. Keep a copy of your report and consult us.
