That notice ("Tiene iniciado un expediente de incapacidad permanente") means that the National Social Security Institute (Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad Social, INSS) has opened the procedure to decide whether you have a permanent disability (incapacidad permanente) and to what degree. It can be opened because you applied for it or because the INSS itself starts it, usually when a period of sick leave (baja) goes on for close to a year or a year and a half. It is not yet a decision: it is the beginning.

> What you do now counts for a lot. Gather the most recent and complete medical reports before you are called in.

## The steps that follow

1. **Summons from the Disability Assessment Team (Equipo de Valoración de Incapacidades, EVI)**, the "medical tribunal" (tribunal médico). They examine you and review your reports.
2. **The EVI's opinion (dictamen)**: it proposes whether there is a disability and to what degree.
3. **The INSS's decision (resolución)**: it recognises a degree, denies it or signs you off as fit for work (alta). The maximum period is 135 days; if it does not reply, it is deemed denied.

## How to prepare

- Take reports from all your specialists, tests and treatments, stating what you **cannot do**.
- Explain your usual job honestly: what tasks you did and why you can no longer do them.
- Do not exaggerate or play things down. The EVI doctors compare what you say with what is on record.

We explain it step by step in [how to apply for permanent disability](/en/blog/how-to-apply-for-permanent-disability).

## If you are on sick leave in the meantime

If the file is opened when your sick leave runs out, ask what happens to your benefit while it is being decided: in many cases the effects of the sick leave are extended until the decision. Your contract does not end automatically: since Law 2/2025 (Ley 2/2025), if you are recognised as having a permanent disability you can ask for your job to be adapted or to be redeployed.

## If it is denied

You have **30 working days (días hábiles)** to file the prior administrative claim (reclamación previa). Many denied disabilities are later recognised in court. Read [what to do if the INSS denies disability](/en/blog/what-to-do-if-the-inss-denies-disability).

## Frequently asked questions

### What does "tiene un comunicado sobre su expediente de incapacidad permanente" mean?

That the INSS has sent you a notification about your file: a summons, a request for documents or the decision. Check it on the Social Security electronic office (sede electrónica) or in the app, because the deadlines run from when it is notified.

### How long does it take to be decided?

Up to 135 days at most. In practice it depends on the province and on whether you are called to the medical tribunal soon.

### Which is better, retiring or total permanent disability?

It depends on your age, your benefit calculation base (base reguladora) and whether you are going to keep working in something else. Total disability (incapacidad permanente total) allows you to work in another profession and, from the age of 55, can reach 75% of the benefit calculation base. It is a good idea to do the numbers with a lawyer before deciding.

### Can you work with total permanent disability?

Yes, in a profession different from the one that you were declared unfit for. We explain it in [degrees of permanent disability](/en/permanent-disability/degrees-of-permanent-disability).
