During the probation period (periodo de prueba), both the company and you can end the contract without notice, without compensation and without giving a reason. But that freedom has limits: the probation period must be agreed in writing, it cannot last longer than allowed and the decision cannot be discriminatory or a reprisal.

## How long it can last

It is set by your collective agreement (convenio colectivo). If the agreement says nothing, the Workers' Statute (Estatuto de los Trabajadores) sets the following maximums:

- **6 months** for qualified technical staff (técnicos titulados).
- **2 months** for everyone else.
- **3 months** for those who are not qualified technical staff in companies with fewer than 25 workers.
- In temporary contracts of up to six months, a maximum of **one month**, unless the collective agreement says otherwise.

If you had already done that same job at the company before, under any type of contract, they cannot give you a probation period.

## When it is not valid

- **It was not in writing** in the contract. In that case there is no probation period and what they did is a dismissal.
- **It had already ended** when they let you go. It is a dismissal and, if there is no cause, an unfair dismissal (despido improcedente).
- **It is discriminatory**: if you are pregnant, the termination is void (nula) unless the company proves that the reason has nothing to do with the pregnancy. The same applies if it is because of your illness, origin or sexual orientation.
- **It is a reprisal**: for claiming your rights or reporting something.

In these cases you have 20 working days (días hábiles) to challenge it, as with any dismissal. [Work out your deadline](/en/tools/deadlines).

## Do I get unemployment benefit?

Yes, if it is the company that ends the contract during the probation period and you meet the other requirements. If you voluntarily left a previous job to start this one, there are nuances: if more than three months have passed since you left the previous job, there is normally no problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do they have to give me a letter?

They do not have to explain the reason, but they do have to tell you that they are ending the contract. Keep the message or the document with the date.

### Do they owe me anything?

Yes, the final settlement (finiquito): the days worked, any holiday not taken and the proportional part of the extra payments (pagas extra). [Calculate it here](/en/tools/final-settlement-calculator).

### What if they let me go the day after I tell them I am pregnant?

The termination is void unless the company proves a reason unrelated to the pregnancy. Read [dismissal during pregnancy](/en/women-at-work/dismissal-during-pregnancy) and do not let the 20 days go by.
