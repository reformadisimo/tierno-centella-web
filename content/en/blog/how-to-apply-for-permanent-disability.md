Permanent disability (incapacidad permanente) is applied for at the National Social Security Institute (Instituto Nacional de la Seguridad Social, INSS), through its online office (sede electrónica) or at a service centre. It can also start on its own, when your sick leave runs out and the INSS opens the file. What is decisive is not the form, but the medical reports showing how your conditions limit your ability to work.

> Before applying, see which degree might apply to you with [the disability checker](/en/tools/permanent-disability-checker).

## Who can apply

- Anyone with serious conditions that are expected to be permanent and that reduce or cancel out their ability to work.
- Usually after a long period of sick leave, although it is not essential to have been on sick leave.
- You must be registered with Social Security (de alta) or in an equivalent situation (situación asimilada) and, if it is due to a common illness (enfermedad común), have contributed for a minimum period that depends on your age. If it is due to an accident, this is not required.

## The steps

1. **Gather the reports**: from your GP (médico de cabecera), from specialists, tests, treatments and their results. The more specific they are about what you cannot do, the better.
2. **Submit the application** to the INSS with your details, a description of your usual occupation (profesión habitual) and the reports.
3. **Disability Assessment Team (Equipo de Valoración de Incapacidades, EVI)**: this is the "medical board" (tribunal médico). They examine you and issue an opinion. Bring all your documents and explain honestly what your day-to-day work is like.
4. **Decision**: the INSS decides whether to recognise the disability and to what degree. If it does not reply within the legal deadline (135 days), the application is deemed refused.

## If your application is turned down

You have **30 working days** from notification to file the **prior administrative claim** (reclamación previa) with the INSS. It is compulsory before going to court, and many refused disabilities are later recognised at the Social Court (Juzgado de lo Social). We explain it in [what to do if the INSS denies your disability](/en/blog/what-to-do-if-the-inss-denies-disability) and in [prior claim and lawsuit](/en/permanent-disability/appealing-a-denied-permanent-disability).

## And your job

Since Law 2/2025 (Ley 2/2025), being granted permanent disability no longer automatically ends your contract: the company must try to adapt your post or move you to another one. Read [your contract after a disability](/en/permanent-disability/permanent-disability-and-your-contract).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a lawyer to apply?

It is not compulsory for the application, but preparing the reports properly from the start prevents many refusals. For the lawsuit, it is advisable to have a specialist lawyer.

### How long does the INSS take to decide?

The maximum period is 135 days. In practice it varies depending on the province.

### Can I apply for it because of anxiety or depression?

Yes, if it is serious and limits your ability to work in a lasting way. Read [permanent disability for anxiety or depression](/en/blog/permanent-disability-for-anxiety-or-depression).

### What degrees are there and how much is paid?

Partial, total, absolute and severe disability (parcial, total, absoluta y gran incapacidad). We explain them in [degrees of permanent disability](/en/permanent-disability/degrees-of-permanent-disability).
