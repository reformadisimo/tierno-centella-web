To challenge a dismissal you have 20 working days from the day after its effective date. Within that time you must file the conciliation claim form (papeleta de conciliación), which in Madrid is handled by the SMAC (Servicio de Mediación, Arbitraje y Conciliación de la Comunidad de Madrid, the Madrid Region's mediation, arbitration and conciliation service). Filing the claim form stops the clock, and after the conciliation attempt you have the days you had not used to file your lawsuit (demanda) at the Labour Court (Juzgado de lo Social).

> If the deadline passes, you lose the chance to challenge the dismissal, even if you were completely in the right. It is a strict time limit (plazo de caducidad) and no excuses are accepted. Work out your deadline with [the deadline calculator](/en/tools/deadlines).

## How the 20 days are counted

They are working days, not calendar days. This means:

- Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays (national, Madrid Region and local holidays where you file) do not count.
- August does count. In dismissal proceedings, August is a working month.
- The days between 24 December and 6 January also count for dismissals.
- You start counting on the day after the effective date of the dismissal, which is the date shown in the letter, not necessarily the day you are handed it.

A simple example: if the dismissal takes effect on a Friday, the first day of the deadline is the following Monday (if it is not a public holiday).

### The same deadline for other cases

The 20 working days also apply to challenging termination for objective reasons (extinción por causas objetivas), collective dismissal (despido colectivo) on an individual basis, and some penalties or decisions by the company. If you want to leave yourself because of serious breaches by the company (termination under art. 50 ET, the Workers' Statute), the deadlines are different.

## Step 1: the conciliation claim form at the SMAC

Before going to court, the law requires you to try to reach an agreement with the company. In Madrid this step takes place at the SMAC.

### What the claim form must include

- Your details and the company's (full name or company name, tax ID (CIF) and address).
- Your job category, length of service and salary.
- The effective date of the dismissal and the reasons the company gave.
- What you are asking for: normally, for the dismissal to be declared void (nulo) or unfair (improcedente).
- If you are also claiming money (wages, holiday), the itemised amount.
- Date and signature.

### Where and how to file it

- Online, through the Madrid Region's Virtual Employment Office (Oficina Virtual de Empleo de la Comunidad de Madrid), with a digital certificate, electronic ID card (DNI electrónico) or Cl@ve.
- In person, at the SMAC registry offices (calle Princesa, 5, and calle Ventura Rodríguez, 7, by appointment), where they usually give you the date of the conciliation hearing on the spot.
- At other official registry offices or by administrative post (correo administrativo). In that case, you will receive the summons by post.

Always keep the receipt showing the date and time you filed it. It is your proof that you filed in time.

> If the claim form is incomplete, the SMAC will ask you to correct it, but that does not stop the deadline. Check it before you send it.

## How the claim form suspends the deadline

Filing the claim form suspends the time limit (art. 65 of the Labour Jurisdiction Act, Ley Reguladora de la Jurisdicción Social). The clock stops that day and starts again:

1. On the day after the conciliation hearing, or
2. If the conciliation hearing does not take place, when 15 working days have passed since you filed the claim form.

Whichever happens first. From then on, you only have the days you had not used up. If you filed the claim form on day 15 of the deadline, you have 5 working days left to file the lawsuit.

If 30 working days pass without the hearing taking place, the step is considered completed and you can go to court.

That is why it is better not to leave it to the last minute. If you file the claim form in the first few days, you will have time to prepare your lawsuit properly.

## Step 2: the conciliation hearing

On the day of the appointment, you (or your lawyer) and the company meet before a conciliation officer (letrado conciliador). This can happen:

- With agreement (con avenencia): you reach an agreement (for example, the company accepts the dismissal was unfair and pays severance). The agreement is enforceable, like a court judgment.
- Without agreement (sin avenencia): there is no agreement and the court route remains open.
- Attempted without effect (intentado sin efecto): the company does not turn up. You can also go to court.

If you are the one who does not attend without a valid reason, the claim form is filed away as if you had never submitted it, and the deadline would keep running.

## Step 3: the lawsuit at the Labour Court

With the conciliation record (acta de conciliación), or the receipt if the hearing did not take place, you file your lawsuit at the Labour Court within the days you have left. Nowadays the lawsuit is normally filed electronically, through a lawyer or a labour relations specialist (graduado social).

The lawsuit must repeat the same facts and requests as the claim form. You cannot add new claims that were not raised at the conciliation stage.

The court then sets the date of the trial. Before the trial there is another attempt at agreement before the court clerk (letrado de la Administración de Justicia).

## Mistakes we often see

- Counting calendar days instead of working days, or thinking August does not count.
- Waiting to see if the company "changes its mind", or negotiating privately while the deadline runs.
- Confusing the effective date with the date the letter was handed over.
- Filing the claim form and forgetting that, after the conciliation, the deadline starts running again.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does August count for challenging a dismissal?

Yes. For dismissals, August is a working month. Only Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are excluded.

### Can I file the claim form myself, without a lawyer?

Yes. You do not have to have a lawyer for the conciliation, but it is a good idea for someone to check what you are asking for and how, because the lawsuit will be limited to what you put in the claim form.

### What happens if the company does not attend the conciliation?

The record is closed as "attempted without effect" (intentado sin efecto) and you can file the lawsuit within the days you have left.

### What if I am also owed money?

Money claims (wages, holiday, overtime) have a one-year deadline (art. 59 ET). You can include them in the same claim form as the dismissal, but you must give the itemised amount.
