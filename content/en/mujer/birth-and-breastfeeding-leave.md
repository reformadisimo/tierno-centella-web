Since 31 July 2025, each parent has **19 weeks** of birth and childcare leave (permiso por nacimiento y cuidado del menor), paid at 100 % of their benefit base (base reguladora) by Social Security (Seguridad Social). Single-parent families can get up to 32. There is also breastfeeding leave (permiso de lactancia), parental leave (permiso parental) and several short periods of leave to care for family members.

> Work out the dates of your leave and how long your job is protected with [the birth leave calendar](/en/tools/birth-leave-calendar).

## The 19 weeks, step by step

Royal Decree-Law 9/2025 (Real Decreto-ley 9/2025) extended the leave from 16 to 19 weeks. They are split like this:

| Period | Weeks | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compulsory | 6 | Straight after the birth, in a row and full time. |
| Flexible | 11 | Until the baby turns 12 months, in a row or by weeks. |
| Up to age 8 | 2 | Whenever you want, until the child turns 8. |

The biological mother can bring forward part of the leave to up to four weeks before the birth. There are extra weeks for multiple births, for the child's disability and for hospitalisation of the newborn.

The flexible weeks must be notified to your employer 15 days in advance.

## Breastfeeding leave

Until your son or daughter turns **9 months**, you are entitled to one hour of absence a day, which you can split into two half-hours, replace with a half-hour reduction at the start or end of your working day, or accumulate into full days if your collective agreement (convenio) or an agreement with your employer allows it. It is a right of each parent and cannot be transferred.

If both parents take it with the same duration and arrangement, the period can be extended to 12 months, with a proportional reduction in salary from 9 months.

## Parental leave

Until the child turns 8, you can ask for up to **8 weeks** of parental leave, in a row or in periods of at least one week, full time or part time. It is currently unpaid, but your job is protected and, according to the Supreme Court (Tribunal Supremo), that time counts towards earning holidays.

## Short leave for caring

- **5 days** for an accident, serious illness, hospitalisation or surgery of a relative up to the second degree or of someone who lives with you. Paid.
- **Up to 4 days a year** for urgent family reasons of force majeure (fuerza mayor), for example your child suddenly falling ill. Paid.

## Your job is protected

While you are taking this leave, and during the 12 months following the birth after your return, a dismissal is void (despido nulo) unless the employer proves a real, unrelated cause. We explain this in [dismissal during pregnancy, birth leave or breastfeeding leave](/en/women-at-work/dismissal-during-pregnancy).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the 19 weeks also for the father?

Yes. Each parent has their own 19 weeks and they cannot be transferred to the other.

### Am I entitled to the 2 new weeks if my child was born before the reform?

The 2 extra weeks to be taken up to age 8 also apply to births from 2 August 2024. It is best to check your specific case.

### Can my employer refuse the dates of the flexible weeks?

As a general rule, no: you give 15 days' notice and take them on the dates you notify. The best-known exception is when both parents work for the same company: it can then limit you both taking them at the same time, if it justifies this in writing for operational reasons.

### Is the 8-week parental leave paid?

As of today, it is unpaid. During full-time leave, your Social Security contributions (cotización) continue.
