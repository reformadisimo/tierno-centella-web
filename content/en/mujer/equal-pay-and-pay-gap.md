Your employer is obliged to pay the same for work of equal value, with no differences based on sex, in base salary and in all pay supplements (complementos). If you earn less than a male coworker who does equivalent work, you can claim the difference for the last 12 months and compensation for discrimination.

> Equal value does not mean the same job with the same title. What is compared is what the work requires: training, responsibility, effort and conditions.

## What the law says

- Article 28 of the Workers' Statute (Estatuto de los Trabajadores) requires equal pay for work of equal value, whatever form the pay takes.
- All companies must keep a **pay register (registro retributivo)** with the average salaries of women and men by group, category or job.
- Companies with an equality plan (plan de igualdad) must also carry out a **pay audit (auditoría retributiva)**.
- If, in a company with 50 or more people, the average of one sex exceeds that of the other by 25 % or more, the company has to justify in the register that the difference has nothing to do with sex.

In 2023 the European Union approved a pay transparency directive that strengthens these rights (individual information about your pay level, a ban on clauses that prevent you from talking about your salary). Its transposition in Spain has been going through the legislative process in 2026; before making a claim, we check what is already in force in your case.

## How to get the information

- If your company has **workers' representatives (representantes de los trabajadores)**, they have access to the full pay register. Ask them for it.
- If there are no representatives, you can ask the company yourself, and it will give you the percentage differences between the averages for men and women.
- Your payslips, your contract, your job description, organisation charts and job adverts for the same position are also useful.

## How it is proved

As in all discrimination cases, it is enough for you to provide indications (indicios): that you do equivalent work and that you earn less. From there, it is the employer that has to prove the difference is due to objective reasons, such as length of service (antigüedad) or a supplement linked to something you do not do.

## What you can claim

1. The pay difference, normally for the last 12 months.
2. To be paid the same from now on.
3. Compensation for moral damages for the discrimination.
4. If the situation is serious and persistent, termination of your contract with the compensation for an unfair dismissal (despido improcedente).

And if you are sanctioned or dismissed for claiming, the measure is void (nula) as retaliation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I ask my coworkers how much they earn?

Yes. Talking about your salary is a right, and a clause that forbids it in order to hide differences based on sex is not valid.

### How many years back can I claim?

Pay differences become time-barred after one year. That is why it is best to claim as soon as you spot it.

### What if my coworker has a different job title?

The title does not matter. What counts is whether the work has the same value because of its real requirements.

### Can I be dismissed for claiming?

No. It would be retaliation and the dismissal would be void (despido nulo).
