If the INSS (Spanish Social Security) refuses your permanent disability (incapacidad permanente) or recognises a lower degree than you believe you are entitled to, you must first file a prior administrative claim (reclamación previa) with the INSS itself within 30 working days of being notified. If the INSS rejects it or does not reply within 45 working days, you have another 30 working days to file a lawsuit (demanda) at the Labour Court (Juzgado de lo Social). At the trial, a judge reviews your case with the medical reports and can recognise the degree the INSS refused.

> Key deadline: 30 working days from when you are notified of the INSS decision. If it passes, you will have to start again and you will lose months of pension. Work it out with [the deadline calculator](/herramientas/plazos) (in Spanish).

## When you can make a claim

You can file a prior administrative claim when the INSS:

- Refuses your permanent disability.
- Recognises a lower degree (for example, total permanent disability instead of absolute).
- Gets the contingency wrong (common illness, enfermedad común, instead of accident at work, accidente de trabajo), or miscalculates your benefit base (base reguladora) or the effective date.
- Lowers or withdraws your degree in a review.
- Does not decide on your application within the time it has to do so. In that case, it is treated as refused by administrative silence (silencio).

## Step 1: the prior administrative claim

The prior administrative claim is a written document addressed to the INSS asking it to change its decision. It is compulsory: without it, the court will not accept your lawsuit.

### Deadline

- 30 working days from the day after you are notified.
- It is an administrative deadline: Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays do not count, but August does.
- If the notification is electronic and you do not open it, it is treated as delivered once 10 calendar days have passed since it was made available to you. Check the electronic office (sede electrónica) and your email.

### What it must include

1. Your details and the case file number (número de expediente).
2. The decision you are challenging.
3. What you are asking for: the specific degree, the correct contingency or the benefit base you consider appropriate.
4. Why: your real limitations for the tasks of your usual occupation, and the errors you see in the EVI report (dictamen del EVI, the medical tribunal).
5. Any medical reports you have, especially if they are new or more complete.

### Where to file it

At the Social Security electronic office (sede electrónica de la Seguridad Social), at an official registry office or at a Social Security Service and Information Centre (Centro de Atención e Información de la Seguridad Social, CAISS). Keep the receipt with the date.

> You do not need a lawyer for the prior administrative claim, but what you put in it shapes the lawsuit. It is best to be specific and to point already to the evidence you will use at trial.

## Step 2: the INSS response

The INSS has 45 working days to reply. It can:

- Accept it and recognise what you are asking for.
- Accept it in part.
- Reject it.
- Not reply. Once the 45 days have passed, it is treated as rejected by administrative silence and you can go to court without waiting any longer.

## Step 3: the lawsuit at the Labour Court

### Deadline

- 30 working days from notification of the rejection, or from when the silence period is complete.
- It is a procedural deadline: here Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays and August do not count, nor do the days from 24 December to 6 January.
- It is a strict time limit (plazo de caducidad): if it passes, the court will apply it even if nobody raises it.

If you miss the deadline, you do not lose your right to the disability, but you would have to apply to the INSS again. As a general rule, payments can only be recovered from three months before the new application.

### What is discussed at trial

In the lawsuit you cannot introduce facts different from those you raised in the prior administrative claim, except those that occurred afterwards. That is why step 1 is so important.

At the trial, the main things assessed are:

- Medical reports from specialists, with diagnosis, treatment and prognosis.
- The link between your limitations and the specific tasks of your occupation.
- The report of a medical expert (perito médico), if one is provided, who can give evidence at trial.
- The INSS case file, including the EVI report.

The judge assesses the injuries you had on the date of the EVI report. If you have got worse since then, it may be better to request a review than to try to bring it into the trial.

### After the judgment

If you do not agree with the judgment, you can appeal to the High Court of Justice (Tribunal Superior de Justicia) (recurso de suplicación). The INSS can also appeal if it loses.

## Determination of contingency

An important part of many claims is not the degree but the origin (determinación de contingencia). If your disability comes from an accident at work or an occupational disease (enfermedad profesional):

- No minimum contribution period is required.
- The benefit base is calculated with your actual wages, and is usually higher.
- There may be a benefits surcharge (recargo de prestaciones) if the company failed to comply with safety measures.

If your sick leave started because of work-related stress, anxiety or depression, for example due to workplace harassment, it is worth checking whether it should be treated as an accident at work. You can read more in [the workplace harassment section](/en/workplace-harassment).

## What you should do

1. Write down the date you were notified of the INSS decision.
2. Ask for a complete copy of your case file, including the EVI report.
3. Talk to your specialists so that their reports describe specific limitations, not just diagnoses.
4. File the prior administrative claim in time and keep the receipt.
5. Count the 30 days for the lawsuit from the reply or the silence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a lawyer for the disability lawsuit?

It is not compulsory at the Labour Court, but it is highly recommended: the trial is technical and is won with well-presented medical evidence.

### Does August count in the deadlines?

For the prior administrative claim to the INSS, yes. For the lawsuit at the court, no.

### Do I have to wait for the INSS to reply to the claim?

No. If 45 working days pass without a reply, it is treated as rejected and your 30-day deadline to file the lawsuit starts to run.

### Can I provide new reports at trial?

Yes, as long as they relate to the injuries you already had when your case was assessed. New conditions usually require a new application or a review.
