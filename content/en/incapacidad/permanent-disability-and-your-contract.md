Since 1 May 2025, when you are recognised as having total permanent disability (incapacidad permanente total), absolute permanent disability (incapacidad permanente absoluta) or severe disability (gran incapacidad), your contract no longer ends automatically. Law 2/2025 of 29 April (Ley 2/2025, de 29 de abril) allows you to ask to stay with the company, and the company must try to adapt your job (reasonable adjustments, ajustes razonables) or move you to another compatible job. It can only end the contract if those adjustments would be an excessive burden, if there is no suitable vacant job or if you turn down the change you are offered.

> You have 10 calendar days from when you are notified of the disability decision to tell the company, in writing, that you want to keep working.

## How it was before and how it is now

Until April 2025, the Workers' Statute (Estatuto de los Trabajadores) said that total permanent disability, absolute permanent disability or severe disability was grounds for ending the contract. In practice, the company could terminate your employment on the same day as the decision, without severance pay.

That clashed with the protection of people with disabilities and with the obligation to make reasonable adjustments. Law 2/2025 changed article 49.1.n) of the Workers' Statute. Now termination is only possible in three cases:

1. When the reasonable adjustments would be an excessive burden for the company.
2. When there is no vacant and available job that matches your profile and is compatible with your new situation.
3. When such a job exists and you turn down the change that is properly offered to you.

## The deadlines

### Your deadline: 10 calendar days

From when you are notified of the decision recognising the disability, you have 10 calendar days to tell the company, in writing, that you want to continue the employment relationship. Do it in a way that leaves a record: burofax (certified letter), email with acknowledgement of receipt or delivery with a stamped copy.

### The company's deadline: 3 months

From that same notification, the company has a maximum of three months to:

- Make reasonable adjustments to your job, or
- Move you to another compatible job, or
- If it considers this is not possible, end the contract, notifying you in writing and explaining the reasons.

In the meantime, your contract is suspended and your job is kept for you.

## What reasonable adjustments are

They are the changes that allow a person with limitations to keep working: adapting the job or the tools, changing tasks, reorganising working hours, providing training or moving to another job. The company's occupational risk prevention service (servicio de prevención de riesgos laborales) must determine the scope of these measures, after consulting the workers' representatives.

### When it is an excessive burden

To decide whether an adjustment is an excessive burden, the following are taken into account: its cost, the size of the company, its resources and financial situation, its turnover and whether there is public funding to offset it.

For companies with fewer than 25 workers, the law is more specific: the burden is only considered excessive if the cost of the adjustment, after deducting public funding, exceeds the higher of these two amounts:

- The severance you would be owed for unfair dismissal (despido improcedente).
- Six months of your salary.

> Note: if the company ends up terminating the contract for this reason, the law does not provide for severance pay. That is why it is so important to check whether there really were no possible adjustments.

## What happens to the pension

If you keep working in the same job with adaptations, or in another job that is incompatible with the pension, your permanent disability pension is suspended while you work in that way. You receive your salary.

If the new job is compatible with the pension (for example, total disability and a job in a different occupation), you can receive both. The law asked the Government to put forward a proposal to review the compatibility between work and pension; until the rules change, these rules apply.

## If the company ends the contract

You can challenge the termination. The law provides that these proceedings are urgent and are given priority. As it does not set its own deadline, the prudent thing is to act as you would for a dismissal and make your claim within 20 working days of the notification. More information in [the guide on the deadline to challenge a dismissal](/en/dismissal/deadline-to-challenge-a-dismissal).

At the trial, the question will be whether the company genuinely considered adjustments, whether there were vacant jobs and whether the burden really was excessive. If the company dismisses you without doing that analysis, or because of your health, there may be discrimination and the dismissal could be void (despido nulo) (read [the guide on void dismissal](/en/dismissal/when-is-a-dismissal-void)).

## And if the disability is partial

Partial permanent disability (incapacidad permanente parcial) has never been grounds for ending the contract. You stay in your job, although the company must take your limitations into account, and in some cases a change of job may be appropriate.

## What you should do

1. When you receive the INSS (Spanish Social Security) decision, write down the date you were notified.
2. If you want to stay, tell the company in writing within 10 calendar days.
3. Ask to be told which adjustments or jobs have been considered.
4. If you are notified that your contract is being ended, keep the letter and make your claim quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Since when does Law 2/2025 apply?

It came into force on 1 May 2025, the day after it was published in the BOE (Spain's Official State Gazette).

### What happens if I say nothing within 10 days?

The law makes continuing in your job conditional on you asking for it. If you do not tell the company in time, it may take it that you do not want to stay and end the contract. If you have missed the deadline, get advice as soon as possible.

### Do I get severance pay if the company cannot adapt my job?

The law does not provide for severance pay for this kind of termination. If the company does not properly justify the excessive burden or the lack of vacancies, you can challenge it.

### Can I receive the pension and my salary at the same time?

It depends on the job. If you stay in the same adapted job or in another job that is incompatible with the pension, the pension is suspended. If the new job is compatible, you can receive both.
